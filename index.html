About

I'm a marketer, communicator and designer based on the NSW South Coast. I've worked in-house, freelance and alongside agencies, on everything from a local folk festival to national retail campaigns.

I work in systems and processes. A clear brief and a clear end point, then I build it properly, with guidelines so it holds together after I've gone.

For corporate, industry and government audiences that's communications. For consumer brands it's marketing. Either way, design is only one part of the job.
